About Me

I'm Anish S. Patil — an engineer from Bangalore making a deliberate transition from QA & Game Testing into the world of AI/ML Engineering.

My testing background gave me something most ML engineers lack early on: a sharp eye for edge cases, systematic debugging instincts, and the discipline to validate models rigorously before calling them "done." Now I bring that mindset to building RAG pipelines, Computer Vision systems, and Generative AI applications.
